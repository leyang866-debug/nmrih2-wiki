## Recognize infection early
Do not wait for a teammate to notice something is wrong. Coughing, visual distortion around the screen, and strange auditory effects are warning signs you should treat seriously. The earlier you call it out, the more time the squad has to find treatment, cover you during a search, or change the extraction plan.

## Slow it down and find treatment
Use **pills** as soon as you need more time, then actively search for **Gene Therapy** rather than hoping the problem will resolve itself. Tell the group what you need and what you have already used. A teammate who knows you are looking for treatment can carry medicine, take the lead through a dangerous room, or decide when the team needs to leave.

## Tell the squad immediately
Keep infection status out in the open. Say when symptoms start, when you take pills, and whether you still need Gene Therapy. Do not split off silently to look for treatment: you are more likely to be trapped, and the rest of the squad loses the chance to protect you.

## Avoid the mistakes that create pressure
You cannot control every close encounter, but you can avoid turning one into a pile-up. Keep a path behind you, avoid unnecessary gunfire, and do not stay locked in melee when several zombies are closing. If the group is low on medicine, play for space and extraction instead of chasing every loot container.

## Treat old transformation advice cautiously
Earlier gameplay accounts describe untreated infection becoming a serious threat to the Responder and team. The exact 1.0 stages and timing can change, so make your decisions from the symptoms you see in the current build: report them early, look for treatment, and extract before the situation overwhelms the squad.
