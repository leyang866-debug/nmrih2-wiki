## Choose Your Map

Objective matches take place on Lewiston, Power Plant, Pottsville, Broadway, Beaulieu Hospital, and Raven Rock. Raven Rock is the sixth Objective map.

Survival matches use Flooded, Night of the Living Dead, and Lighthouse. These three Survival maps arrived with 1.0.

## Broadway Route

A Broadway run can follow this sequence:

1. Restore power by starting the generators. A mistake while operating one can reset it.
2. Find the frequency booklet and tune the radio to the listed frequency.
3. Search drawers and cabinets for hard drives, then upload the data three times.
4. Move through CRC safehouses, including the fire station and grocery store. Seal doors and windows as you pass through.
5. Travel through the subway tunnel to Broadway.

## Pick an Extraction Plan

Broadway can end in two ways. For helicopter extraction, add fuel to the signal bonfire and survive until the helicopter arrives. For train extraction, find the keys, clear the tracks, restore power, and board the train.

Objectives can vary, so treat this as a route to recognize rather than a guaranteed checklist. Tell the team which extraction you are working toward before committing supplies or time.

## Practice Before the Real Run

Use Solo Training to learn landmarks, objectives, and exits on Objective and Survival maps. Training does not give normal progression or permanent character loss, making it the place to test a route before taking a progressing Responder into a match.
