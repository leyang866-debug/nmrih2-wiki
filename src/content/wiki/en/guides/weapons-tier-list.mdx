## Best ranged weapons
These ratings reflect community 1.0 gameplay experience. Use them to decide what to keep when inventory is tight, then choose for your route, ammunition, and squad role.

| Weapon | Rating | Strengths | How to use it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| M781 | S | Full-auto, attachment support, 20-round magazine, headshots can drop normal and red zombies in one shot | Take it in solo or co-op when you need one gun that can clear pressure quickly. Fire controlled bursts and leave yourself a retreat path. |
| M14 Battle Rifle | A | Hard-hitting semi-auto rifle with a 20-round magazine | Use it for accurate priority kills and medium-range pressure. It is a strong solo choice when you can land shots instead of spraying. |
| MP5 | A | Flashlight, attachment support, and excellent crowd control | Keep it near the front of a co-op group. Use it to protect a teammate or hold back a close horde, but do not waste its ammo on a single isolated zombie. |
| MC-15 | A | 30-round magazine, high accuracy, reliable stopping power | Use it when you expect repeated contacts and want more shots before reloading. It rewards calm, deliberate fire. |
| M1911 | A | Best-ranked handgun; one headshot can kill | Carry it as an ammunition-efficient solo sidearm or a reliable backup when your main gun runs dry. |
| 590A1 shotgun | A | Seven-round magazine and powerful close-range stopping power | Use it at doorways, tight corridors, and teammate rescue range. Do not bring it into an open fight without a second option. |
| Rochester 1873 | B | High damage | Make each shot count. The slow reload and small magazine make it risky when several zombies close at once. |
| Hunter 85 | B | High damage | Treat it as a deliberate single-target weapon. Reload only after creating space or while teammates cover you. |
| Model 13 revolver | C | Starter weapon; one headshot can kill a normal zombie | Use it until you find an upgrade. Six rounds disappear quickly, so do not use it as your only answer to a horde. |
| Ferrera DB12 | C | Very high damage and can remove limbs | Its two-round capacity makes it a close-range emergency tool. Fire, create distance, then reload safely. |
| M93A3 | C | Reliable 9mm, 15-round magazine, flashlight and suppressor support | It is dependable but often needs two headshots. Use it for careful cleanup, not as your main crowd-control weapon. |

## Best melee weapons
The melee ranking below comes from Early Access community testing. 1.0 rebalanced melee attack speed and damage, so treat the ordering as a practical starting point and re-test a favorite weapon after patches.

| Weapon | Rating | Strengths | How to use it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Baseball Bat | S | Fast swings, efficient stamina use, good reach, consistent stagger control | The safest all-round melee pick. Use the reach and knockback to keep a small group in front of you instead of trading hits at point-blank range. |
| Fire Axe | A | Huge damage; charged attacks can kill a normal zombie quickly | Charge it only when you have room. A missed swing costs stamina and leaves you exposed during recovery. |
| Tree Trimmer | A | Very long reach and limb removal | Use it to stay outside grab range and turn dangerous enemies into crawlers before they reach the team. |
| Sledgehammer | A | Strong spacing tool with long reach | Use it like the tree trimmer: create distance first, then commit to a swing. |
| Hand Axe | B | High damage, sensible swing speed, low punishment for mistakes | A reliable early find that can stay with you for a full run. Use it when you need a balanced, forgiving weapon. |
| Cleaver | B | Reliable edged option and effective limb removal | Use it to reduce a group's mobility, then back away instead of trying to finish every target in place. |
| Shovel | B | Good reach, damage, and multi-zombie control | Strong once you learn its slower swing rhythm. Spend stamina carefully and avoid committing in cramped rooms. |
| Kitchen Knife | C | Very fast | Its short reach and low damage force you too close. Use it only when you have no safer alternative. |
| Red Bar | C | Better than a one-handed starter tool | A temporary two-handed choice; replace it when you find a weapon with better reach, damage, or control. |
| T-Junction Pipe | C | Basic two-handed option | Works in a pinch but lacks the strengths that make the better melee choices dependable. |
| Giant Wrench | C | Usable damage | It looks stronger than it feels. Replace it when a bat, axe, trimmer, or shovel appears. |
| Small Pipe | D | Starter weapon | Replace it as soon as possible; it takes too many hits to finish targets safely. |
| Tire Iron | D | Slightly faster than the small pipe | The short reach still keeps you in danger. Use it only until you loot a real upgrade. |
| Wrench | D | Average all-round performance | It has no standout advantage. Swap it out when you find any higher-tier control tool. |
| Mallet | D | Some stopping power | Slow swings and short reach make it awkward in close quarters. Do not rely on it when retreat space is limited. |

## Choose for your run
- **Solo:** M1911 and M14 are strong when you need to preserve ammunition and make clean, accurate kills. Pair either with a bat, hand axe, or other safe melee backup.
- **Co-op:** M781, MP5, and 590A1 are excellent for protecting teammates and controlling a close horde. Let one player hold the close-range role while others cover distance and reload windows.
- **Any mode:** A high tier does not fix bad positioning. Reload behind cover, avoid shooting into a fight you can route around, and do not trade your last magazine for one zombie.
