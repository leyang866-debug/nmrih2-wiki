## Armageddon 1.0 Wiki
Armageddon ends Early Access and adds or expands Survival Mode, Raven Rock, Solo Training, tutorial content, PS5 and Xbox support, and full crossplay.

## New Modes and Maps Guide
Survival uses Flooded, Lighthouse, and Night of the Living Dead. Raven Rock is the sixth Objective map. Solo Training provides practice without normal progression or permanent character loss.

## Progression and Economy Guide
Weekly Assignments, Credits, Merits, expanded customization, and the Rescue Beacon all give you more reasons to plan a run beyond extraction. Check your active assignments before queuing, then build the run around a task you can realistically complete with the squad. In-game values can change with patches, so check the current menus before spending Credits or rerolling.

## Perks, combat, and difficulty
The update added reload-focused perks, removed three older perks, and reduced the reroll cost. It also changed melee, firearms, ammunition, sprinting, shoving, and difficulty. Relearn your usual loadout after the update instead of assuming an Early Access favorite still has the same timing or stamina cost.

## Reset and launch context
Some pre-1.0 account, Credit, and Responder progression was reset at launch, while Early Access rewards remained. If you are returning, review your Responder and inventory before your first serious run, then use Solo Training to get comfortable with the revised systems.
