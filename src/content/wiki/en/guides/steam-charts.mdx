## Check the Current Steam Player Count

Steam player counts change constantly. For the latest number, check SteamCharts or SteamDB directly. Steam data covers Steam players only, not players on Epic, PS5, Xbox Series X|S, or the whole crossplay community.

## Early Access Peak

The recorded Early Access peak was about 11,000 concurrent Steam players. This is a historical high, not a current player count.

## 2026 Monthly Averages Before 1.0

The following Steam averages were recorded before the Armageddon 1.0 release:

- **April 2026:** about 130 players
- **May 2026:** about 1,001 players during an event
- **June 2026:** about 210 players
- **July 2026:** about 157 players

## Do These Numbers Show 1.0 Interest?

No. Armageddon 1.0 released on August 11, 2026, after all of the monthly figures above. They cannot measure the 1.0 launch, its first-week retention, or the current population.

## Use Player Counts Wisely

Use Steam numbers to spot Steam-specific trends over time, not to judge every platform's activity or whether you can find a co-op group. Crossplay means the total active community is larger than the Steam count alone.
