## Pick perks for the job you do

When you level up, choose one of three perks. Pick the option that improves what you expect to do most often: fight with a particular weapon, carry supplies, save stamina, or support teammates. A perk that matches your usual job is more useful than a random damage bonus.

## Build around your squad

If your team needs supplies, choose carrying or ammunition perks when they appear. If you often revive teammates, prioritize revive support. If you rely on a specific weapon type, take handling, range, accuracy, damage, or reload perks for that weapon rather than splitting your picks across unrelated roles.

## Perks seen at the 1.0 launch

Players reported perks for heavy rifles, melee, recoil, hip-fire accuracy, environmental damage, carrying capacity, handguns, kicks, revives, ammunition pickups, shotgun range, and quiet movement. Examples include **Mule** for more carrying capacity, **Staying Alive** for faster revives, and **Scavenger** for larger ammunition pickups.

These observations are from the 1.0 launch period, so the available pool and effects may change with balance updates.

## 1.0 perk-pool changes

Version 1.0 added **Handgun Reloading**, **Rifle Reloading**, and **Shotgun Reloading**. It removed **Averaging**, **Guts**, and **Teddy Bear**, and reduced the cost to reroll perks.

## Keep your choices focused

Before choosing, ask what caused trouble in your last run. Take stamina support if melee leaves you exposed, reload support if you lose openings while reloading, or scavenging support if your squad runs short on ammunition. Change direction only when your role in the squad changes.
