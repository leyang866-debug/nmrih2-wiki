## Play as a Responder

You control a Responder, not a fixed hero class. Each Responder has independent progression and perks, so the character you bring into a match represents progress you can lose.

## Let Progress Shape Your Decisions

Responder death affects the progression cycle. Do not treat a dangerous objective as a free retry: decide with the team whether the likely reward is worth putting a developed character at risk. Extract when the situation stops being worth that risk.

## Make the Character Your Own

Armageddon 1.0 expanded avatar and character customization. Use the available customization options to set up the Responder you want to play, but do not expect a traditional roster of fixed combat-role heroes.

## Coordinate Around the Character at Risk

Before pushing an objective, call out who needs supplies, who is carrying important gear, and whether the squad should keep advancing or extract. A coordinated retreat can preserve a Responder's progress for the next match.
