## Is NMRIH2 on Xbox?

Yes. *No More Room in Hell 2* is available for Xbox Series X|S and supports Xbox Play Anywhere. It launched on consoles with Armageddon 1.0 on August 11, 2026.

## Can Xbox Players Use Crossplay?

Yes. Xbox Series X|S players can squad up with players on PC and PS5. Update the game before joining friends so everyone is playing the current version.

## Xbox Price and Online Play

The listed US launch price was $29.99, with a 20% launch discount. Prices and offers change, so check the Microsoft Store for your region.

Online multiplayer requires an Xbox subscription. Check the Microsoft Store listing for the current subscription requirement before you buy.

## Before You Join a Squad

- Confirm that each player is on PC, PS5, or Xbox Series X|S.
- Install the latest game update.
- Make sure your Xbox account has access to online multiplayer.

## Performance Details

No console performance test is included here. Check the current Microsoft Store listing and in-game settings for the latest Xbox Series X|S information.
