## Practice alone before a co-op run

Solo Training was added in version 1.0. You can enter every map alone to learn the layout, understand objectives, and get comfortable with weapons and movement before joining a squad.

## Learn Objective and Survival maps

Use Solo Training to find key routes, landmarks, exits, and places where you can retreat. You can also practice Objective and Survival maps, making it useful when you want to understand a mode without risking a co-op run.

## Set one goal per session

Keep practice focused. You might learn an objective route, find an extraction point, test a weapon, or rehearse a safe path through an area. Once you know one part of a map well, practice another instead of trying to memorize everything at once.

## Know what does not carry over

Solo Training is practice, not a full solo progression mode. It has no normal progression and no permanent character loss. Use it to improve your next multiplayer run, not to farm the regular economy.

## Put the knowledge to work

When you return to co-op, tell your squad about useful routes, supplies, danger points, and extraction options. Map knowledge helps the team move with purpose and avoid getting separated.
