## No More Room in Hell 2 Release Timeline

- **October 22, 2024:** Early Access began.
- **August 11, 2026:** Armageddon 1.0 released, ending the Early Access period after about 22 months.
- **August 11, 2026:** The game also launched on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, the series' first console release.

## What Armageddon 1.0 Means for Players

Armageddon 1.0 marks the full release. It includes Survival Mode, Raven Rock, Solo Training, a tutorial, crossplay, console support, perk changes, and difficulty adjustments.

## Where Can You Play?

You can play on PC through Steam and Epic, as well as on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. The August 11, 2026 console launch arrived at the same time as the 1.0 PC release.

## Price at Release

The listed standard US price was $29.99. Check your platform store for the current regional price and any active discount.

## Planning a First Session

Release dates do not confirm an exact unlock time for every region. If you are coordinating a squad, check the store page and your platform's local availability first.
