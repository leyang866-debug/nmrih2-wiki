## Defend the Speakers

Survival Mode is a second game mode added in version 1.0. Your squad protects **Speakers** that attract zombies, so stay close enough to defend the objective while keeping clear sight lines and a route out.

## Prepare for timed waves

Each wave lasts five minutes. Before a wave starts, check where enemies can approach, share ammunition, and decide who watches each direction. Do not let the whole squad chase the same group while another route is left open.

## Plan for extraction

After three waves, your team can extract by helicopter. If your team fails two waves, you can extract early. Call the decision clearly: stay only if you can still defend the next wave and reach the helicopter together.

## Pick a defensible position

Use space to retreat, avoid blocking teammates, and keep your strongest weapons for the busiest approaches. Cover the objective first; kills away from the Speaker matter less if zombies reach it unchecked.

## Survival maps

The dedicated Survival maps are **Flooded**, **Lighthouse**, and **Night of the Living Dead**. Use Solo Training to learn their routes and defensive areas before putting a permanent Responder at stake in co-op.
