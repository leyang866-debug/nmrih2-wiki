## Is NMRIH2 on PS5?

Yes. *No More Room in Hell 2* launched on PS5 on August 11, 2026, alongside Armageddon 1.0. This was the series' first console release.

## Can PS5 Players Use Crossplay?

Yes. PS5 players can play with people on PC and Xbox Series X|S. That gives your squad more ways to fill a co-op session across the three supported platforms.

## PS5 Price at Launch

The listed launch price was US$29.99, €29.99, or £24.99, with a 20% PlayStation Store launch discount. Store prices and discounts can change by region and over time, so check the PlayStation Store before you buy.

## Before You Join Friends

- Make sure every player owns the game on PC, PS5, or Xbox Series X|S.
- Update the game before attempting to join a cross-platform session.
- Confirm your PlayStation account can use online multiplayer.

## What Is Not Confirmed Here

No PS5-specific 30 FPS or 60 FPS target, graphics-mode list, or performance test is available here. Check the current PlayStation Store listing and in-game options for the latest details.
