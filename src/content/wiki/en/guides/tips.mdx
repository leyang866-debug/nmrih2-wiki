## Stay with the main squad
Do not chase the fastest player and do not take a solo route just because it looks shorter. Follow the main group so you can call out danger, share ammunition, cover reloads, and pull each other out of a bad corner. A player who runs ahead can bring a horde back to the group; leave enough space to react without losing sight of the team.

## Save ammunition by avoiding bad fights
You do not need to kill every zombie. Route around a group when the objective allows it, because gunfire and running can bring more zombies into the area. Use a car only as a short breathing space—zombies can climb it. Look for obstacles they cannot climb, then use that separation to complete an objective, heal, reload, or move on.

## Carry the gear you will actually use
Backpacks have both weight and space limits. Before picking something up, ask whether it helps the current objective or the next fight. Leave duplicate low-value items behind so you have room for ammunition, healing, a better weapon, or a key objective item.

- Hold **U** to unload ammunition from a gun before you discard or replace it.
- Pick up supplies when you find them: they do not add weight and can earn credits for later runs.
- Share a useful weapon or ammunition with a teammate who is under-equipped instead of hoarding duplicates.

## Manage light, doors, and objectives
With a two-handed weapon equipped, use **Q** or the flashlight key to switch to your light and press it again to return to the weapon. Turn the flashlight off when you leave a dark room unless you have spare batteries.

Press **C** whenever you lose the route. It shows your compass direction and the current objective. For doors, try kicking white or reinforced doors; blue doors and iron grates do not open this way, so stop wasting time and find another route.

## Treat infection and downed zombies as active threats
Coughing, visual distortion, or strange sounds can mean infection. Use pills to slow it down, look for gene therapy to cure it, and tell your teammates immediately if you cannot treat it. Keeping it secret turns a medical problem into a squad emergency.

A zombie on the ground is not automatically dead. If it is still making noise, give it space and be ready for it to rise. Use a shove for a brief safe moment, or hold the shove input for a more effective kick when you need to create room. Two teammates can alternate this control while the rest of the squad moves or finishes the objective.

## Loot with a plan
Open drawers, cabinets, refrigerators, and other containers instead of only checking obvious floor loot. Some areas behind bars can contain safes. Loot quickly, call out important finds, and keep moving before the noise and delay turn a quiet search into a crowded fight.
