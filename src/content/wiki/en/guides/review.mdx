## Quick Verdict

*No More Room in Hell 2* may suit you if you want tense co-op survival built around scavenging, objectives, and the danger of losing a character permanently. Its August 15, 2026 Steam snapshot was Mixed, so weigh the rough edges against the kind of co-op experience you want.

## Steam Review Snapshot: August 15, 2026

On August 15, 2026, Steam reviews were roughly 49% positive and 51% negative. That number is only a snapshot from shortly after the 1.0 launch, not a permanent verdict. Check current Steam reviews before buying.

## Common Player Concerns

Early Access criticism often focused on performance and optimization, melee feel, animations, and matchmaking. Reports around the console launch also mentioned optimization, controller deadzone, and settings not saving.

These are reported player concerns, not a guarantee that every player will have the same experience.

## What Players Liked

Early feedback after 1.0 included clear positive reactions. Players highlighted the pressure of co-op survival, looting, completing objectives, and the stakes created by permanent character loss.

## What Changed With 1.0?

Armageddon 1.0 added Survival Mode, Raven Rock, Solo Training, tutorial content, console support, crossplay, perk changes, and difficulty adjustments.

## Should You Buy It?

Choose it for coordinated co-op survival and high-stakes runs. Wait or check current platform reviews if responsive melee, smooth performance, and stable matchmaking are your top priorities.
