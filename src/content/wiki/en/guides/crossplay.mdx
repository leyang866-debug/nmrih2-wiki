## Play Across Platforms

No More Room in Hell 2 supports full crossplay across PC, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X|S. Players on any of those platforms can join the same co-op group.

## Build the Squad You Need

Co-op supports up to eight players. Platform choice does not need to split your regular group, so build the squad around who is available instead of separating players by system.

Before a match, agree on a route, a regroup point, and who will carry important supplies. A larger squad gives you more players to cover the group, but it also needs clear calls when the team moves, searches, or extracts.

## Use Crossplay for 1.0 Co-op

Crossplay is part of the 1.0 console release. If your group includes PC and console players, bring everyone into the same session and focus on the match rather than choosing a platform-specific party.
