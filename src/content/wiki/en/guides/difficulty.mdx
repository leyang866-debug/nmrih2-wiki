## Choose a difficulty your squad can finish

NMRIH2 has four difficulty levels: **Beginner**, **Normal**, **Hard**, and **Nightmare**. Pick a level that lets your team complete objectives and extract consistently. Higher difficulty can offer different XP and Credit rewards, but a failed run does not help your progression.

## Start with Beginner

Beginner is the best place to learn the game. It gives new players a more forgiving revival system, so you can practice navigation, objectives, combat, and extraction with less pressure.

## Use Normal for the standard experience

Normal is the standard setting. Move here once you can stay together, manage ammunition, and finish objectives without relying on Beginner’s extra forgiveness.

## Expect pressure on Hard

Hard puts more pressure on your squad and makes resources tighter. Save ammunition, avoid unnecessary fights, and agree on an extraction plan before committing to major objectives.

## Treat Nightmare as a team challenge

Nightmare is the highest difficulty. You face stronger zombies, scarcer resources, and a reduced UI. Bring a coordinated squad, communicate early, and leave room to retreat instead of fighting every group you encounter.

Patch changes can alter exact values, so move up only when your squad can consistently manage the added pressure.
