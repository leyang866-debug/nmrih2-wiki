## NMRIH2 Wiki Guide
No More Room in Hell 2 is an eight-player co-op survival horror FPS by Torn Banner Studios. Armageddon 1.0 released on 2026-08-11 for PC, PS5, and Xbox Series X|S with crossplay.

## Core Survival Guide
Manage resources, control noise, respond to infection risk, and extract before a run consumes your Responder. Standard US pricing in the supplied material is $29.99.
